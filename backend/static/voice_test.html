Nivaran voice test

How to test (you play the customer, the bot plays customer care)
  1. Pick a language, press Start, allow the microphone.
  2. Wait for the bot to say hello (the blue "Bot said" line appears).
  3. Say your problem like a real customer. Then stop and wait for the bot to answer: it asks one question.
  4. Answer the question. After 1 or 2 questions it tells you what it understood. It never sends anything.
